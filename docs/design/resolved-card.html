Shorthand marks

2 min hands-on becomes ● 2m. 30–40 min · walk away becomes ◷ 30–40m. Roughly a 70% cut in character count, which also fixes a real clipping bug — and drops a label that the bar underneath was already saying.

Why the labels had to go

The old cell put a long right-aligned string above a fixed-width glyph. In a 4.6rem column the string overflowed its leading edge and was silently clipped — “2 min hands-on” rendered as “n hands-on”. Worse, it was redundant: solid fill versus hatch on the bar below already carried the same distinction.

Beforeclipped · redundant

melt2 min hands-on
bake 350°F30–40 min · walk away

21 characters in a cell that fits about 12. Right-aligned overflow clips at the left edge, so the surviving fragment is the least informative part of the string.

Afterfits · marks the exception only

melt2m, 2 minutes hands-on
bake 350°F30–40m, 30 to 40 minutes, unattended — you can walk away

Six characters. Fits at every column width in the corpus, including the 4.6rem columns in shepherd's pie. Full text is preserved for screen readers.

The mark vocabulary

Three marks total. The rule that keeps it small: label the exception, not the default. Most steps need your hands — that's unremarkable and gets the quiet dot. What a cook actually needs to spot from across the kitchen is the step that frees them.

MarkMeansFromRead aloud as
2m Hands-on. The default state. effort: 'quick' | 'minutes' “2 minutes hands-on”
30–40m Unattended. You can walk away. effort: 'long-unattended' “30 to 40 minutes, unattended — you can walk away”
— Duration not recorded. Bar renders as a dashed empty track. duration: undefined “duration not recorded”

Duration shorthand

2m · 45m · 1h · 1h30 · 30–40m (en dash, no spaces) · <1m. Mono, tabular figures, so digits align down a column. No unit words — the unit is unambiguous at this scale and the word is the expensive part.

Icons are decorative in the accessibility tree (aria-hidden); every mark carries a visually-hidden full sentence, so nothing is lost to a screen reader. That's the Phase 06 contract: the compression is visual only.

What this changes in the plan

Phase 03 gains a mark vocabulary in packages/tokens — three symbols, a duration formatter, and the rule that icons are aria-hidden with a visually-hidden sentence alongside. formatDuration() joins formatQuantity() and formatTemperature() in core, so every experiment track renders 30–40m identically rather than inventing its own.

Phase 06 gains an explicit acceptance criterion: the shorthand must read as full prose to a screen reader. Visual compression must never reach the accessibility tree.

And a caution worth recording — marking only the exception assumes unattended steps stay rare. They do across this corpus (one per recipe), but a braise or a bread recipe could be mostly waiting, at which point the clay clocks stop being an accent and become the field. Check against a long-ferment recipe before treating this as settled.
