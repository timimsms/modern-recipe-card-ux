# License and provenance — the recipe corpus

The JSON files in this package are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (CC BY 4.0).

**What these files are.** Transcriptions of recipe *facts and structure*: ingredients,
quantities, step topology, timings and temperatures, in this project's authoring form. Under US
practice, ingredient lists and procedures are uncopyrightable facts; expressive prose is not
transcribed. Step text is kept terse and functional, and where a source's wording was expressive
it has been rephrased. Each file credits its source in a `source` field, and adaptations say so
(see `bbq-pulled-chicken.json`, cooked differently from its printed source on purpose).

**What is not here.** Source photographs, scans and PDFs are not distributed with this
repository, and contributions that reproduce a source's expressive text will not be accepted —
see `CONTRIBUTING.md` at the repository root.

This is an engineering-judgment summary, not legal advice.
